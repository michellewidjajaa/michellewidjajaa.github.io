U-Shop

Chairperson

Running a student merchandise store, working with partners and helping our sales support student bursaries.

S$12K+10th Anniversary campaign revenue
3.5×Year-on-year sales growth
Nearly 4×Prior daily peak on launch day
70%Of profits go towards student bursaries

The responsibility

At U-Shop, 70% of profits go towards student bursaries. That gave every campaign a purpose beyond selling merchandise. We needed to understand what students wanted, work within our resources and make sure we could deliver what we promised.

My role

I worked with partners, designers and the store team from early discussions through to launch and restocking. Student polls and feedback helped us decide what to make and when to bring it back.

  • Led the adidas × NTU collaboration from discussion to launch.
  • Brought designers, publicity and operations teams together.
  • Used polls and student feedback to guide designs, demand and restocks.
  • Kept student bursaries in mind when making sales and campaign decisions.

What changed

The adidas × NTU launch delivered record-high first-day sales at nearly four times the previous daily peak. The 10th Anniversary campaign brought in more than S$12,000 and achieved 3.5× year-on-year sales growth. With 70% of profits going towards student bursaries, these sales also helped support the student community.

What I learned

I learned how much a campaign depends on the work around the idea. Listening to students, getting teams on the same page and checking the practical details all mattered when it was time to launch.
