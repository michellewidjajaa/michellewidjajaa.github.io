The journey

As Project Director for BK 2026, I helped plan and run our environmental projects and support for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). For BK 2027, I’m stepping into the role of Vice-President Internal, helping four divisions coordinate their work.

BK 2026 impact

Our work in Boyolali combined environmental infrastructure with practical support for local enterprises and farmers.

Environment

Building wells and planting trees in Desa Cluntang

We built 13 absorption wells in Desa Cluntang, Boyolali, and planted a total of 125 trees there.

MSME development

Helping local products and businesses grow

We created an AI and Digital Marketing Workshop for small businesses in Desa Cluntang. We also ran a cream-cheese-making workshop for local farmers in Boyolali, exploring how cow's milk could be transformed into a higher-value product.

How BK is organised

Balik Kampoeng is organised into four divisions: Project, Business, PnP and IT. As Vice-President Internal, my role is to help them coordinate their plans for our work in Indonesia.

  • Help Project, Business, PnP and IT agree on priorities and responsibilities.
  • Support each division as it turns plans into activities.
  • Check that our plans reflect what the community needs.
  • Work out what we can deliver with the time and resources we have.

Before Indonesia

Before the main project in Indonesia, volunteers usually take part in smaller service activities in Singapore or Batam. These “Beloem Balik Kampoeng” activities give volunteers a chance to practise working together and learn from the people they meet before the main project.

The leadership challenge

For me, community work starts with listening. I need to understand what people need, help the team work together and be realistic about what we can offer. That’s the approach I want to bring to BK 2027.
